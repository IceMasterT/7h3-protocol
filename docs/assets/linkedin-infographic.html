E2E Encryption
X25519 key exchange + ChaCha20-Poly1305 AEAD. Forward secrecy per message. Zero new deps.

Capability Tokens
Scoped, time-bounded delegation. Agent A grants B specific routes. B delegates narrower scope to C.
NEW

Stream Signing
Per-chunk HMAC + final Ed25519 over full content hash. Tamper detected mid-stream.
